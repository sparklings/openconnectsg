# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **OpenConnectSg** community a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free, and inspiring experience for everyone—regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity, technical background, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are here to solve tricky Windows networking problems, automate the tedious stuff, and build clean, reliable open-source tooling together.

---

## The Vibe: Keep It Cool, Keep It Pragmatic

We believe open source thrives when people communicate openly, act with kindness, and focus on practical engineering. Here are the core ground rules:

### 👍 What We Champion
* **Empathy and respect**: Treat fellow developers and users with dignity. Disagreements are normal—debate ideas and technical trade-offs rigorously, but never attack the person.
* **Curiosity and welcoming energy**: Everyone was a beginner once. Welcome questions, provide constructive feedback, and share knowledge freely.
* **Pragmatism over bureaucracy**: We value working solutions and clean automation. No unnecessary gatekeeping or dogmatic red tape.
* **Focus on the collective win**: We prioritize what is best for the project and the users relying on uninterrupted VPN connections.

### 🚫 What We Don't Tolerate
* **Harassment and abuse**: Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
* **Gatekeeping and condescension**: Talking down to newcomers or dismissing bug reports without constructive explanation.
* **Inappropriate behavior**: Unwelcome sexual attention, harassment, or sharing private details without explicit consent.
* **Disruptive conduct**: Spamming, bad-faith arguments, or persistent toxic behavior.

---

## Enforcement & Handling Issues

We are pragmatic engineers, not bureaucrats. Most everyday misunderstandings can be resolved through a quick, polite conversation in the thread.

However, if serious or persistent misconduct occurs, project maintainers will step in fairly, transparently, and without drama:

1. **Correction**: A private or gentle public clarification when someone steps slightly out of bounds.
2. **Warning**: A formal notice when behavior crosses into hostility or disruption, with clear expectations for improvement.
3. **Temporary Cool-Down**: A temporary ban from participating in issues, discussions, or pull requests.
4. **Permanent Removal**: Permanent ban for deliberate harassment, hate speech, or bad-faith sabotage.

---

## Scope & Reporting

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests, commit comments) and whenever representing **OpenConnectSg** in public forums.

If you encounter unacceptable behavior or have a concern, please report it via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/sparklings/openconnectsg/security/advisories) or directly contact the repository maintainers. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
